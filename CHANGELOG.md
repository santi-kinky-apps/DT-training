# Changelog

## v1.2.0

- **Faster double taps:** a double tap now asks for both taps in a beat and a half, instead of two full beats.
- **Countdown beeps:** in the last 3 seconds of every hold (Keep it in and Rhythm), beeps that get higher and higher let you know you're almost there without looking at the screen.
- **Time editor:**
  - It now also lets you change the Rhythm pace (seconds between beats) for every day of every level.
  - Changing only the Rhythm pace doesn't reset your progress.
  - Orders that impose your editor times include the Rhythm pace too.

## v1.1.0

- **Rhythm is easier and more realistic.** Each beat now gives you time to pull the toy all the way out and take it back in.
  - The beat interval starts at 5 s on Rookie mouth and slowly goes down across the 28 days, never below 3 s.
  - You can tap at any point before the next beat.
  - Double taps last two beats.
  - After every hold there's a 5-second rest, with a countdown, before the next beat.
- Custom Rhythm intervals are now 3, 4, 5 or 6 s.

## v1.0.0

First public release.

- **Three daily tests:** Keep it in, Rhythm and Quick Tap. A day is complete when all three are done.
- **Program:** 4 levels × 7 days (Rookie mouth, Trained throat, Pro throat, Insatiable throat).
  - **Hardcore** has its own progress (double reps, +5 s per rep).
  - **Master mode** unlocks after finishing all 4 levels in Hardcore.
- **Modifiers:** Perfect training, Continuous training, Safe mode (20 s cap) and Real pace.
- **Seconds editor:** adjust the length of every rep. It also sets the Rhythm hold.
- **Tasks:**
  - Orders with unique IDs, created by one person and received by another through a code.
  - Tasks are ticked off automatically, with an alert for each one.
  - Daily tasks, surprise tasks, deadline countdown and automatic punishment.
  - Optional settings lock while the order lasts.
  - Sent-orders list for whoever creates them.
  - An order can't be deleted while it has tasks in progress.
- **Achievements**, a training calendar and a streak counter.
- **Backup code** to move progress between devices or browsers.
- Installable on the home screen as **Rutina**, and works offline.
- English and Spanish, light and dark themes, three color palettes.
